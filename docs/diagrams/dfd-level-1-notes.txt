Palermo DFD Level 1 — Notes

Purpose
-------
This diagram decomposes the single Palermo process from DFD Level 0 into six
major logical processes and seven logical data stores. It remains technology-neutral:
the stores are logical groupings, not physical PostgreSQL schemas or Prisma models.

Level 1 processes
-----------------
1.0 Account & Profile Management
2.0 Catalogue, Discovery & Virtual Fragrance
3.0 Personalisation, Quiz & AI Support
4.0 Cart, Order, Payment & Delivery
5.0 Community, Loyalty & Referral
6.0 Administration & Operations

Logical data stores
-------------------
D1 Customer & Profile Data
D2 Catalogue & Fragrance Data
D3 Commerce & Fulfilment Data
D4 Inventory & Production Data
D5 Quiz, Recommendation & Support Data
D6 Community & Loyalty Data
D7 Admin, Promotion & Audit Data

External entities
-----------------
- Visitor
- Customer
- Administrator
- Email delivery service
- Stripe sandbox / payment service
- AI service / API

Important boundary and balancing rules
--------------------------------------
- External entities match DFD Level 0.
- The internal delivery simulator remains inside Palermo.
- No raw card number/CVV flow enters a Palermo data store.
- Stripe returns provider references/status only.
- AI receives minimised approved context and is not authoritative for catalogue,
  pricing, stock, orders, payment, delivery or policy facts.
- Public catalogue and Visitor cart/quiz/support behaviour remains available without
  authentication where approved.
- Order placement, own-order access and customer-specific support require Customer
  authentication/ownership.
- Administrative activity remains subject to server-authorised RBAC.
- The Level 1 stores intentionally group the detailed entities frozen in the ERD/data
  dictionary; they do not replace the detailed data model.

Next decomposition
------------------
DFD Level 2 should decompose Process 4.0 — Cart, Order, Payment & Delivery because it
contains the highest-risk transaction boundary: checkout revalidation, bounded stock
reservation, payment verification, idempotency, inventory commitment/release, invoice
creation and shipment initiation.
