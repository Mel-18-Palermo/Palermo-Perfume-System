Palermo system-wide use-case diagram — SRS Section 6

Purpose
-------
This is the report-level system use-case overview. It deliberately groups the detailed domain use
cases already stored under docs/srs/use-cases/ so the diagram remains readable in the Final SRS.
It does not replace the detailed use-case specifications or their FR/DER traceability.

Canonical actors
----------------
Business actors:
- Visitor
- Customer
- Administrator

External systems:
- Email delivery service
- Stripe sandbox / payment service
- AI service / API

The baseline delivery simulator is internal to Palermo and is therefore shown inside the system
boundary rather than as an external actor.

Important scope boundaries
--------------------------
- Public catalogue/discovery and virtual-fragrance functionality is available to Visitor and Customer.
- Visitors may use a temporary cart, but order placement requires an authenticated active Customer.
- Wishlist is customer-specific.
- Personalisation, layering, sample-set, quiz and public AI support flows may be used by Visitor or Customer where allowed by the detailed use cases.
- Customer-specific account/order/delivery support requires authentication and ownership checks.
- Administrative functions require server-authorised RBAC.
- No real courier actor is included in the baseline.
- AI is supporting only; Palermo remains authoritative for catalogue, payment, order, inventory and policy facts.
