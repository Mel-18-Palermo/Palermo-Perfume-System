Palermo Checkout / Payment Sequence Diagram — SRS Section 9

Purpose
-------
This is the primary Final SRS sequence diagram for the authenticated checkout,
payment and order-finalisation journey. It is intentionally aligned with:
- UC-ORDER-001 Place Order
- UC-PAY-001 Process Online Payment
- UC-DELIVERY-001 Select Delivery Method
- DFD Level 2 Process 4.0 decomposition

Participants
------------
Customer
Palermo Web UI
Palermo Application
Authoritative Palermo Data
Stripe Sandbox
Internal Delivery Simulator

Important constraints represented
---------------------------------
- Order placement requires an ACTIVE authenticated Customer; no guest checkout.
- Browser/client price and total values are not authoritative.
- Checkout revalidates current catalogue, promotion, delivery and stock information.
- Stock is not reserved while merely in the cart.
- A bounded inventory reservation is created only during checkout/payment.
- Order and payment state are stored separately.
- Order items, pricing, addresses, discount and delivery information are snapshotted
  at order placement.
- Customer card credentials are supplied directly to Stripe's provider-controlled
  payment surface and are not stored by Palermo.
- Palermo verifies the provider payment result server-side before applying a
  successful business outcome.
- Successful payment commits inventory once, records the attributable inventory
  movement once, creates the invoice once and starts one shipment workflow.
- Failed/expired/abandoned payment releases the reservation safely.
- Duplicate checkout submissions and provider callbacks resolve to the existing
  business outcome rather than duplicating orders, payment confirmations, stock
  changes or invoices.
- The delivery simulator is internal to Palermo; it is not a real external courier.
