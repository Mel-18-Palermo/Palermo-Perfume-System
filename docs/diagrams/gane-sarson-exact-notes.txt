Palermo exact Gane & Sarson DFD presentation artefacts

Why this patch exists
---------------------
Mermaid's `shape: rounded` is suitable for the rounded process outline, but
Mermaid's `shape: datastore` is documented as a datastore with top/bottom borders.
That is not the exact Gane & Sarson data-store symbol.

The report-ready SVGs added by this patch therefore use explicit Gane & Sarson
presentation symbols:
- process: rounded rectangle with a process-number header band;
- external entity: rectangle;
- data store: right-open rectangle with a left identifier compartment;
- data flow: labelled directional arrow.

Canonical/source relationship
-----------------------------
The existing `.mmd` files remain the repository's editable logical source and preserve
the approved flows. The matching `*-gane-sarson.svg` files are the presentation
artefacts to embed in the Final SRS when exact Gane & Sarson notation is required.

Files

- docs/diagrams/dfd-level-0-gane-sarson.svg
- docs/diagrams/dfd-level-1-gane-sarson.svg

No requirement, actor, process scope, data-store meaning or trust boundary is added
by these presentation artefacts.
