Palermo DFD Level 2 — Commerce / Order / Payment / Delivery

Purpose
-------
This diagram decomposes DFD Level 1 Process 4.0, "Cart, Order, Payment & Delivery",
into its transaction-level logical processes. It is the detailed DFD used for
Final SRS Section 8.2.

Sub-processes
-------------
4.1 Manage Cart
4.2 Validate Checkout
4.3 Reserve Inventory & Create Order
4.4 Process & Verify Payment
4.5 Finalise Paid Order & Invoice
4.6 Manage Order & Cancellation
4.7 Manage Shipment & Tracking

Parent-process balancing
------------------------
The Level 2 diagram preserves the Level 1 Process 4.0 boundaries:
- Visitor interacts only with temporary cart functionality.
- Customer interacts with cart, authenticated checkout, payment/result, order,
  cancellation and shipment/tracking functions.
- Stripe is the only external payment service.
- The delivery simulator remains internal to Palermo.
- D2, D3, D4 and D7 are the same logical stores used by Process 4.0 at Level 1.

Critical transaction rules represented
--------------------------------------
- Visitor can maintain a temporary cart but cannot place an order.
- Client/browser prices and totals are never authoritative.
- Checkout revalidates variant availability, prices, customisations, promotion
  eligibility, delivery information and stock before order placement.
- Adding to cart does not reserve stock.
- Inventory reservation is bounded to checkout/payment and must be atomic.
- Order/item/address/delivery/discount values are snapshotted at placement.
- Order and payment lifecycle states remain separate.
- Stripe card credentials are handled by the provider-controlled payment surface;
  Palermo stores/verifies provider references and status, not raw PAN/CVV.
- Repeated submissions/provider callbacks must not duplicate orders, payment
  confirmation, inventory effects or invoices.
- Payment failure/expiry releases the bounded reservation safely.
- Successful verified payment commits reserved inventory exactly once and creates
  the invoice exactly once.
- Cancellation is a request for an eligible pre-shipment own order; no refund
  behaviour is invented.
- One baseline shipment exists per order.
- Tracking and delivery confirmation come from the internal simulator workflow.
