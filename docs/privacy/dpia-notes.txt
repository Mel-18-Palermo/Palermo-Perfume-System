Palermo DPIA package notes

This package implements D-097 through D-102 without introducing a new D-number.

It deliberately distinguishes:
- project/SRS privacy controls from unperformed legal/compliance review;
- account deactivation from data deletion;
- active-store retention from rolling backup retention;
- Palermo-controlled data from external-provider processing.

Retention periods are project baseline controls intended to make the SRS testable and
bounded. They are not represented as statutory accounting, taxation or legal retention
periods. A production provider/legal review is explicitly required before launch if the
client needs different periods.
